# Awesome-Fundraising-Platform

## Top Fundraising Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Online Donations, Peer-to-Peer Campaigns, Recurring Giving, Events & Nonprofit Fundraising*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Fundraising**. These systems help nonprofits and causes accept online donations, run peer-to-peer and event campaigns, manage recurring giving, and engage donors.



**Examples** include Classy (GoFundMe Pro), Givebutter, Donorbox, Fundraise Up, Give Lively, OneCause, Qgiv (Bloomerang Fundraising), Neon Fundraise, Bonterra Network for Good, and JustGiving (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured commercial fundraising platforms dominate the nonprofit market. Practical open options include donation-page platforms, CiviCRM extensions, and community fundraising tools. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the commercial gap for payments, compliance, and scale.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Classy / GoFundMe Pro](https://www.classy.org/)**  

  Enterprise fundraising platform (now under GoFundMe Pro) for sophisticated campaigns, peer-to-peer, events, and advanced analytics aimed at larger nonprofits.



- **[Givebutter](https://www.givebutter.com/)**  

  All-in-one fundraising platform popular for peer-to-peer, social campaigns, events, and free core usage with optional tips or paid plans.



- **[Donorbox](https://donorbox.org/)**  

  Widely used donation platform offering embeddable forms, recurring giving, text-to-give, crypto/stock options, and a broad integration catalogue.



- **[Fundraise Up](https://fundraiseup.com/)**  

  Conversion-optimized donation platform focused on high-volume online giving, smart forms, and performance-driven fundraising.



- **[Give Lively](https://www.givelively.org/)**  

  Mission-aligned, free-to-use fundraising platform for US nonprofits with low processing costs and campaign tools.



- **[OneCause](https://www.onecause.com/)**  

  Fundraising and event platform specializing in auctions, galas, peer-to-peer, and mobile bidding for nonprofits.



- **[Qgiv / Bloomerang Fundraising](https://www.qgiv.com/)**  

  Online fundraising tools (now part of Bloomerang) for donations, events, and peer-to-peer campaigns integrated with donor management.



- **[Neon Fundraise / Neon CRM](https://www.neoncrm.com/)**  

  Fundraising and CRM platform for nonprofits covering online giving, events, membership, and constituent management.



- **[Bonterra Network for Good](https://www.bonterratech.com/)**  

  Nonprofit technology suite including fundraising, donor management, and engagement tools under the Bonterra umbrella.



- **[JustGiving](https://www.justgiving.com/)**  

  Popular peer-to-peer and crowdfunding platform widely used for individual and charity fundraising campaigns.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[CiviCRM](https://github.com/civicrm/civicrm-core)**  

  Leading open-source CRM for nonprofits with strong fundraising, contribution, event, and membership modules; highly extensible for campaigns and donor management.



- **[Happiness](https://github.com/heysanil/happiness)**  

  Open-source donation pages platform built with Next.js—supports custom donation pages, Stripe checkout, and donor tracking.



- **[Donazy](https://github.com/kodingworks/donazy)**  

  Open-source donation and fundraising management platform (Laravel) for creating campaigns, tracking targets, and managing contributions.



- **[Giver](https://github.com/giverio/giver)**  

  Open-source responsive fundraising platform concept supporting multiple organizations, projects, rewards, and Stripe donations (early-stage).



- **[Odoo / ERPNext nonprofit and donation modules](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open ERP modules that can be adapted for donation tracking, campaigns, and basic nonprofit fundraising workflows.



- **[Stripe + open form open integrations](https://github.com/)**  

  Community examples of self-hosted donation forms and checkout flows using Stripe or similar processors.



- **[Peer-to-peer campaign open prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Experimental tools for running personal fundraising pages and team campaigns without a commercial platform.



- **[Recurring giving and subscription open engines](https://github.com/)**  

  Open billing libraries usable for monthly donor programs when combined with payment processors.



- **[Event and auction open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Community projects for ticketing, simple auctions, and event registration that can support fundraising events.



- **[Documentation and nonprofit open playbooks](https://github.com/)**  

  Guides for building transparent, low-cost fundraising stacks with open tools and direct payment processing.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **CiviCRM** as the core for donor management, contributions, and campaign tracking with full data ownership.

- Deploying **Happiness** or similar open donation-page platforms for branded giving experiences.

- Combining open CRM with direct Stripe/PayPal processing to minimize platform fees.

- Accepting that polished peer-to-peer experiences, high-conversion optimized forms, mobile bidding, compliance tooling, and large-scale event support still favor commercial platforms (Givebutter, Donorbox, Fundraise Up, Classy/GoFundMe Pro, OneCause, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, lower fees, and mission-aligned transparency.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Manage donors and campaigns in CiviCRM or an open CRM → host donation pages with open tools or simple Stripe Checkout → process payments directly → report and receipt via open or commercial tools. Suitable for small-to-mid nonprofits with technical capacity. Larger organizations and high-volume campaigns typically rely on commercial fundraising platforms for conversion, support, and integrated features.
